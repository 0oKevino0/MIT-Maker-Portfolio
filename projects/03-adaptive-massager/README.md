# Adaptive massager design

[Back to MIT Maker Portfolio](../../README.md)

I lead the massager design and development.

## Design tools and materials

My [claude-cad](https://github.com/0oKevino0/claude-cad) workflow connects Claude Code with Python/CadQuery/OCP for parametric CAD construction, geometry checks, and export. I used this workflow in the massager design and SOLIDWORKS for assembly. The portfolio's CAD evidence PDF shows the design output; the repository documents the modeling workflow. Cura was used for print preparation.

The claude-cad repository is my MIT-licensed modeling workflow used for this project, rather than a separate fourth portfolio project. I used my open-source claude-cad project for CAD modeling. The CAD includes simplified reference geometry for an ESP32 DevKit V1, N20 motors, and a Li-ion battery.

## Development status

The device remains in development. Pressure sensing and physiological feedback are design goals, and I am coordinating fabrication with an external manufacturer. The linked workflow and separate CAD evidence document the design; they do not establish completed fabrication, validated feedback control, or clinical effectiveness.
